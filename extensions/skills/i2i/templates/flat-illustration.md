A clean flat-style vector illustration inspired by the reference image.
Simplify the main subject into bold geometric shapes with smooth curves and
flat color fills — no gradients, no outlines, no shading. Use a harmonious
palette of 4-6 muted complementary colors that match the mood of the
original photo. Place the subject on a soft solid-color background with a
subtle drop shadow. The composition should feel modern, minimal, suitable
for a brand mascot or editorial illustration. Do not add extra decorative
elements. Preserve the subject's recognizability at small sizes.
