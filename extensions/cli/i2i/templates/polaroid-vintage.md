A Polaroid-style photograph of the subject in the reference image. Keep the
main subject recognizable but apply a warm, slightly faded vintage color
grading — soft yellows in highlights, muted teals in shadows. Add a subtle
light leak from the top-right corner (warm orange/red). Include a thin white
Polaroid border with a slightly wider bottom margin. The overall mood should
feel nostalgic, candid, like a memory from the late 1990s. Do not stylize
the subject itself — preserve natural skin texture and realistic proportions.
Avoid heavy shadows or dramatic contrast.
